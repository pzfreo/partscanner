Partsong

Scan choral sheet music on your phone and practise your own part, with the other parts quieter.

Everything runs on your device: the music is read on the phone, and your scores stay on it unless you share them. No account needed.

When you share a score, an encrypted copy is uploaded so the link can bring it to the people you send it to. The key is only in the link, so nobody else, Partsong included, can open it. Copies are deleted after a year.

To see which features get used, Partsong counts visits and actions (such as reading a score or pressing play) anonymously with GoatCounter: no cookies, nothing that identifies you, and never your scores or their names.
